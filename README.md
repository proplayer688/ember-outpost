## 🌟 Executive Summary & High Concept

**EMBER OUTPOST: SIEGE & DEFENSE** reimagines what a Web3 gaming experience can be. While typical hackathon entries rely on static menu-clicking, luck-based coin flips, or repetitive spinning-dial timing minigames, Ember Outpost delivers a **full-fledged real-time isometric combat and base-building experience**:

- **Real-Time Fortress Demolition:** Plan drop vectors, monitor tactical energy reserves, and deploy autonomous assault squads (Volt Imps, Pulse Rangers, Obsidian Breachers, and EMP Overcharge strikes) to breach fortified AI bastions. Watch defense towers rotate, blast plasma bolts, lob mortar shells, and collapse into smoking debris.
- **Active Base Defense Warfare:** Switch from the attacker to the defender. Fortify your perimeter with Pulse Turrets, Tesla Coils, and Energy Barriers, sounding the alarm to repel incoming enemy assault waves in real time.
- **Living Settlement & Wilderness:** Take control of **Ignis the Keeper**, exploring a handcrafted 2.5D isometric settlement. Walk between harvestable timber groves, aether crystal fissures, obsidian outcrops, and the Grand Forge.
- **True Economic Tension ($RF Token Sinks):** Every assault unit deployed burns simulated $RF directly out of the economy, demanding genuine tactical skill to achieve profitable 3-star conquests. 100% deterministic math with zero roulette or dice rolls.

### Pillar 1: Tactical Siege Assault (Offense)
- **Deployment Drop Perimeter:** Players deploy units in the tactical drop zone outside the fortress's red boundary line.
- **Energy Meter Dynamics:** Units require combat energy (1 to 4 energy). Energy recharges smoothly at 1.0 energy/sec (max 10), rewarding disciplined wave timing over chaotic button mashing.
- **Autonomous Tactical AI:** 
  - Units calculate line-of-sight paths toward defensive towers, barrier walls, and the enemy's Central Core.
  - Defending turrets acquire targets within their firing radius, leading shots with plasma projectiles and mortar arcs.
- **Destruction Rating & 3-Star Mastery:**
  - ⭐ **1 Star:** Achieve 50% fortress destruction.
  - ⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Destroy the rival's fortified Central Core.
  - ⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** 100% Total Base Annihilation (Unlocks maximum pillage loot +36 Trophies).

### Pillar 2: Active Base Defense (Defense)
- **Inbound Assault Raids:** When your outpost grows in value, rival factions launch retaliatory strike waves against your territory.
- **Defensive Grid:** Position Pulse Turrets, Tesla Coils, and Mortar Batteries around your Outpost Core and Token Vault.
- **Dynamic Wave Combat:** Watch your defensive battery track and eliminate marching enemy swarms, protecting your stored reserves and earning defense bounties.

### Pillar 3: Living Settlement & Wilderness Exploration
- **Character Locomotion:** Freely guide **Ignis the Keeper** across the outpost with responsive WASD keyboard controls or on-screen mobile touch analog stick.
- **Resource Harvesting:** Interact with natural resource nodes:
  - 🌲 **Timber Grove:** Yields refined lumber for perimeter scaffolding.
  - 💎 **Aether Crystals:** High-energy mineral used in unit spell charges and core expansion.
  - 🪨 **Obsidian Outcrop:** Dense volcanic rock needed for armored wall reinforcement.
- **Grand Forge Smelting:** Transmute raw harvest into Brass Ingots and Aether Alloys to upgrade your settlement from an *Ember Hearth (Level 1)* to a *Brass Citadel (Level 3)*.

## 🤖 The Four Rival Citadels

Players can launch tactical assaults against four unique AI rival strongholds, each engineered with distinct layouts, defensive doctrines, and loot tables:

1. **Sentinel Vex — "The Iron Bastion"**  
   - *Theme:* Heavy frontal defense with twin Pulse Turrets and layered barrier chokepoints.  
   - *Strategy:* Deploy Obsidian Breachers to smash the outer gates while Pulse Rangers snipe turrets from the flanks.  
   - *Loot Pool:* 120 Coins · 35 $RF · +24 Trophies.

2. **Architect Milo — "The Spire of Aether"**  
   - *Theme:* Concentric crystal maze guarded by a central Tesla Coil and rapid-fire defenses.  
   - *Strategy:* Time an EMP Overcharge strike on the Tesla Coil just as your Volt Imp swarm reaches the inner courtyard.  
   - *Loot Pool:* 180 Coins · 50 $RF · +28 Trophies.

3. **Artificer Nova — "The Clockwork Foundry"**  
   - *Theme:* Long-range Mortar Battery perimeter backed by fortified high-durability walls.  
   - *Strategy:* Spread units across multiple drop angles to avoid clustered mortar shell explosions.  
   - *Loot Pool:* 250 Coins · 75 $RF · +32 Trophies.

4. **Envoy Cleo — "The Sunken Sanctum"**  
   - *Theme:* The ultimate citadel featuring overlapping fire from Pulse, Tesla, and Mortar emplacements.  
   - *Strategy:* Coordinated multi-wave assault requiring full energy management, EMP suppression, and balanced squad composition.  
   - *Loot Pool:* 380 Coins · 110 $RF · +36 Trophies.

## 💎 Token Activity & Economic Tension ($RF Mechanics)

Ember Outpost is specifically designed for the **Token Activity & Economy Potential** track. The economy is built around **real economic tension** where every combat deployment and settlement upgrade directly burns or sinks tokens:

### The Mathematical Loop:
```
[ Harvest & Idle Yield ] ──▶ [ Mustering Assault Squads ] ──▶ [ 100% Deterministic Combat ]
          ▲                               │                               │
          │                               ▼                               ▼
[ Base Expansion Sinks ] ◀── [ Token Sinks ($RF Burn) ]   [ 3-Star Conquest Loot Pool ]
```

### Economic Sinks & Burns:
| Economic Action | Cost | Economic Mechanism | Impact on Circulating Supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deploy Volt Imp** | 10 $RF | Combat Munitions Fee | **Burned permanently** upon deployment |
| **Deploy Pulse Ranger** | 20 $RF | Energy Weapon Charge | **Burned permanently** upon deployment |
| **Deploy Obsidian Breacher** | 35 $RF | Demolition Armor Foundry | **Burned permanently** upon deployment |
| **Cast EMP Overcharge** | 25 $RF | Aether Lightning Conduit | **Burned permanently** upon deployment |
| **Upgrade Base Defense** | 50 $RF + 200 Coins | Fortification Blueprint | Sunk into Outpost defense infrastructure |
| **Upgrade Outpost Core** | 100 $RF + 500 Coins | Citadel Tier Advancement | Sunk into settlement progression |

### 100% Deterministic & Non-Gambling:
- **Zero RNG & Zero Gambling:** There are no dice rolls, slot machine spins, or roulette wheels. All unit damage, projectile flight times, turret firing rates, and demolition scores are governed by transparent mathematical formulas.
- **Skill-Based Outcomes:** Whether you earn 1, 2, or 3 stars is 100% dependent on your deployment timing, drop angles, and tactical spell targeting.

## 🎨 Audio & Visual Craftsmanship

- **100% Procedural Web Audio Synthesis:**  
  The game contains **zero external MP3 or WAV audio files**. Every sound effect and musical chiptune arpeggio is generated procedurally in real time using the browser's native **Web Audio API**:
  - *Laser Bolts:* Rapid exponential frequency drops on triangle wave oscillators.
  - *Mortar Launches & Explosions:* Low-frequency square waves blended with white noise buffer generators.
  - *Tesla Zaps:* Frequency-modulated saw-tooth oscillators simulating high-voltage arcs.
  - *Demolition Crashes:* Multi-stage resonance filters simulating stone and metal collapse.
  - *Star Earned Fanfare:* Melodic major arpeggios on sine oscillators.
- **100% Procedural Pixel Textures:**  
  All character sprites, units, defensive structures, projectiles, and environmental nodes are procedurally rasterized onto HTML5 canvases at initialization. Zero external sprite sheet dependencies, ensuring instantaneous loading even on slow 3G mobile connections.
- **Cyberpunk-Fantasy Neon Palette:**  
  Styled in accordance with Rare Friends aesthetic identity:
  - Neon Lime (`#22C55E`) · Cyan Plasma (`#38BDF8`) · Ember Amber (`#F59E0B`)
  - Coral Rose (`#F43F5E`) · Royal Violet (`#8B5CF6`) · Deep Void (`#070A13`)
- **Retro CRT Shader:**  
  Subtle scanline and vignette overlay rendering a classic arcade monitor feel.
