## 🌟 Executive High Concept

**EMBER OUTPOST: SIEGE & DEFENSE** is an isometric tactical strategy and real-time combat experience. Moving beyond static menus and repetitive QTE timing dials, the game delivers **visceral real-time attack and defense warfare**:

- **Siege Assaults:** Deploy squads of Volt Imps, Pulse Rangers, and Obsidian Breachers to breach and dismantle rival AI fortresses (*Sentinel Vex's Iron Bastion*, *Architect Milo's Spire*, *Artificer Nova's Foundry*, *Envoy Cleo's Sanctum*). Watch defense towers rotate, blast plasma bolts, lob mortar shells, and collapse into smoking rubble.
- **Active Base Defense:** Fortify your Outpost perimeter with Pulse Turrets, Tesla Coils, and Energy Barriers. Trigger the **[ 🚨 INBOUND RAID ]** alarm to repel marching enemy assault legions in real time.
- **Living Settlement Exploration:** As **Ignis the Keeper**, walk through your outpost, harvest Timber, Aether Crystals, and Obsidian, smelt trade alloys, and upgrade your Outpost Core from Ember Hearth to Brass Citadel.
- **100% Deterministic & Non-Gambling:** Outpost development, unit stats, demolition scores, and bounties are purely mathematical and skill-based—zero dice rolls, zero roulette, zero casino mechanics.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls
| Platform | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Desktop / Laptop** | `WASD` or Arrow Keys to Walk · `[E]` to Interact / Harvest / Talk · Mouse Click to Deploy Units / Cast Spells |
| **Mobile / Phone** | Virtual Analog Thumbstick to Walk · `[ ACT ]` Button to Interact · Tap anywhere outside the red perimeter to deploy units |

### Combat Deck (Tactical Siege Mode)
- ⚡ **Volt Imp (Cost: 1 Energy / 10 $RF):** Fast dual-blade swarmer. Attacks nearest structures.
- 🎯 **Pulse Ranger (Cost: 2 Energy / 20 $RF):** Long-range sniper. Fires laser bolts over walls at defensive turrets.
- 🛡️ **Obsidian Breacher (Cost: 3 Energy / 35 $RF):** Heavy demolition tank. Deals 2x damage to barrier walls and turrets.
- 💥 **EMP Overcharge (Cost: 4 Energy / 25 $RF):** Tap anywhere on the enemy base to call down an electric lightning storm that disables turrets for 4.0s and deals 110 area damage.

### Destruction & Star Rating
- ⭐ **1 Star:** 50% Fortress Destruction
- ⭐⭐ **2 Stars:** Citadel Core Demolished
- ⭐⭐⭐ **3 Stars:** 100% Total Annihilation (+36 Trophies & Max Loot)

## 🚀 Quick Start / How to Run Locally

Because the project is 100% self-contained with procedural assets and Web Audio, you can run and play it with a single command:

```sh
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Run local development server
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser!

### Testing on Your Phone
When running `npm run dev -- --host`, Vite displays your Network URL (e.g. `http://192.168.1.X:5173`). Open that URL on your phone's browser (Safari/Chrome) to play instantly with full touch controls and 60 FPS!
